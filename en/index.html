Ordering from abroad and delivery in Tallinn

You can order a funeral wreath or funeral bouquet online from another country. Choose the product, add the funeral ribbon message and provide the delivery date, time and address.

After receiving your order, we contact you during business hours to confirm the order and clarify any necessary details.

We deliver in Tallinn and Harju County to homes, chapels, churches, cemeteries, funeral ceremonies and other agreed addresses.

We recommend placing the order at least 2–3 days in advance whenever possible. For urgent orders, contact us and we will let you know what can be arranged.

Funeral ribbons and choosing the right arrangement

You can add your own message to the funeral ribbon. A short, personal and respectful message usually works best.

If you are not sure what to write, we can help you choose suitable wording in Estonian or English.

If you are unsure which wreath or bouquet is appropriate, we can advise you based on the ceremony, location, size and colour preference.
