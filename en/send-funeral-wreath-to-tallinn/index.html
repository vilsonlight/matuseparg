Who is this service for?

This service is for people who are outside Estonia but want to send funeral flowers to Tallinn. It is suitable for relatives, friends, colleagues, companies and organisations who cannot attend the funeral in person.

Delivery to a family home

If the flowers should arrive before the funeral, we can deliver a wreath or bouquet to a family member's home or another agreed private address in Tallinn.

Delivery to a chapel, church or crematorium

We can deliver directly to the place where the farewell ceremony is held. Provide the name of the chapel, church, crematorium or venue together with the date and approximate time.

Delivery to the funeral or cemetery

If you want your flowers to be present at the funeral itself, we can bring them to the ceremony or cemetery at the agreed time.

A message from you on the ribbon

Your ribbon message can include the name of the deceased, a personal farewell and the name of the person or family sending the flowers. If you are unsure about Estonian wording, we can help.

Ordering from another country

You can arrange the order remotely. Choose the flowers online, send us the delivery details and ribbon text, and we will confirm the order with you before preparing it.

Can I order from abroad if I am not in Estonia?

Yes. This service is specifically suitable for customers who are outside Estonia and want to send funeral flowers to Tallinn.

You can choose the product online and provide the funeral date, delivery address, preferred time and ribbon message.

After receiving the order, we contact you to confirm the details before preparing the arrangement.

Where can you deliver the funeral wreath or bouquet?

We deliver in Tallinn and Harju County.

Possible delivery locations include a family home, funeral home, chapel, church, cemetery, crematorium, funeral ceremony, memorial service or another agreed address.

If you are unsure what address to use, send us the information you have and we will help clarify the delivery details.

How early should I place the order?

We recommend placing the order at least 2–3 days before the funeral whenever possible.

For urgent orders, contact us and we will tell you what can still be prepared and delivered in time.

For international orders, payment can be arranged by bank transfer. Customer service is available Monday–Saturday, 9:00–18:00 Estonian time.
