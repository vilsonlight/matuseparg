    Ordering and delivery

    After you submit the order, we will contact you during business hours to confirm it and clarify any necessary details.

    If the order is urgent, we recommend calling +372 53 42 3572 or +372 50 93 229.

    We recommend ordering a funeral bouquet 3–5 days in advance when possible. This gives us the best choice of flowers and enough time to prepare the arrangement carefully.

    For urgent orders we will always do our best, but with very short notice some flowers may not be available.

    We deliver funeral bouquets in Tallinn and Harju County at the agreed time, including to homes, chapels, churches, cemeteries and funeral ceremonies.

    Orders are delivered by our own courier so we can keep better control of the delivery time and the condition of the flowers.

    Funeral ribbons and order information

    A funeral ribbon message can be added to the bouquet. The message should usually be short, sincere and respectful.

    Examples: “In loving memory of our dear grandmother” or “Rest in peace. With love from your family.”

    A standard-length ribbon message costs €5. For a longer message, smaller lettering is used and the price is €10.

    The ribbon text is written by hand in professional calligraphy.

    If needed, we can help you choose a suitable funeral bouquet based on the ceremony, location, size and overall style.

    Quality and care of the funeral bouquet

    We use carefully selected flowers and pay attention to both the technical and visual quality of every arrangement.

    Our aim is for the funeral bouquet to suit the dignity of the ceremony and retain its appearance as well as possible.

    Keep the flowers in a cool room, away from direct sunlight and heat.

    During transport, the bouquet should not be compressed or have objects placed on top of it.

    In cold weather, avoid sudden temperature changes before the ceremony.

    Complaints are handled within 24 hours after delivery.
