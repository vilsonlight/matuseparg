Fir-branch funeral wreaths

A fir-branch funeral wreath is a traditional and dignified choice for a final tribute. The wreath is made on a metal ring using fir branches, with fresh flowers arranged in floral foam. It is suitable for family members, relatives, friends, colleagues and companies.

    Ordering and delivery

    After you submit the order, we will contact you during business hours, usually within 1.5 hours, to confirm the order and clarify the details.

    We recommend placing the order at least 2–3 days in advance. For urgent orders, we will do our best, but the choice of flowers may be more limited.

    The wreaths are prepared by Florist Jakobson and delivered to the agreed address in Tallinn or Harju County.

    We can deliver to a home, chapel, church, cemetery, funeral ceremony or another agreed address.

    If you have not heard from us, please call +372 53 42 3572.

    Funeral ribbon and order details

    The ribbon message is usually short and sincere. For example: “In loving memory of our dear grandmother” or “Rest in peace. With love from your family.”

    A standard-length ribbon message costs €5. If the text is longer, smaller lettering may be needed and the final price will be confirmed with the order.

    If needed, we can help you choose a suitable funeral wreath based on the ceremony, location, size and overall style.

    Quality and care

    We use carefully selected flowers and pay attention to both technical and visual quality.

    Flowers are delicate, and the condition of the wreath also depends on storage and transport.

    The wreath should be carried from underneath by the wreath ring and should not be compressed from the sides or top.

    Keep the wreath in a cool place, away from direct sunlight and heat.
